Project overview

A practical introduction to inventory workflows.

This project is designed to help a small shop keep its product and stock information consistent. Product, purchase, and sale records are stored in a database, while stock quantities change as purchases and sales are recorded.

Before completing a sale, the system checks that the product exists and that enough stock is available.

1

Create a product

Save the product name and price, then create its stock record.

2

Receive inventory

Record a purchase and add its quantity to the current stock.

3

Make a sale

Check availability, calculate the total, and subtract sold items.

4

Review stock

Display the catalog alongside each product’s current quantity.
